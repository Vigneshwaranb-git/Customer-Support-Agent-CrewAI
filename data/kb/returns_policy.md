# Returns Policy
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.
To start a return, go to My Orders > Select item > Request Return.

# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
Orders above 999 INR ship free.
